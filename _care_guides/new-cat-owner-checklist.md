Bringing a new cat into your home is exciting — but it can also feel overwhelming if you're not sure what they actually need. Here are 10 essentials every new cat owner should know, based on real experience raising Millie.

## 1. Give Them Time to Adjust
Cats are territorial by nature, and a new environment can be stressful. Set up a small, quiet room with food, water, a litter box, and a hiding spot, and let your cat explore the rest of the home gradually over several days.

## 2. Litter Box Basics
The general rule is **one litter box per cat, plus one extra**. Place boxes in quiet, low-traffic areas — cats are surprisingly picky about privacy.

## 3. Choose the Right Food
Kittens, adult cats, and senior cats all have different nutritional needs. Look for food that lists a real protein (like chicken or fish) as the first ingredient, and avoid excessive fillers.

## 4. Scratching Is Natural — Redirect, Don't Punish
Cats scratch to mark territory and keep their claws healthy. Instead of discouraging it, provide a sturdy scratching post near their favorite off-limits spots.

## 5. Learn Basic Body Language
- **Tail low and puffed** = fearful or defensive
- **Slow blink** = trust and affection
- **Flattened ears** = anxious or annoyed

Understanding these cues helps you respond to your cat's needs before stress builds up.

## 6. Schedule a Vet Visit Early
Even if your cat seems healthy, an early wellness check establishes a baseline and ensures vaccinations/parasite prevention are up to date.

## 7. Invest in Play, Not Just Toys
Interactive play (wand toys, laser pointers used responsibly) matters more than the toy itself — it builds trust and burns off energy, especially for indoor cats.

## 8. Cats Need Vertical Space
Shelves, cat trees, and window perches satisfy a cat's instinct to observe from above, and can reduce anxiety in multi-pet households.

## 9. Grooming Isn't Just for Long-Haired Cats
Regular brushing reduces shedding and hairballs, and it's a great way to build a bonding routine — even short-haired cats benefit.

## 10. Patience Is the Real Secret
Every cat adjusts at their own pace. Some warm up in days, others in weeks. Consistency and gentle routine matter more than rushing the process.
