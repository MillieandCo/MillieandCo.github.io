Welcome to Millie & Co. — a cat care and lifestyle site built by cat lovers, for cat lovers.

This site is named after our own mischievous muse, Millie, who inspired the whole idea. Whether you're a first-time cat owner or a longtime cat parent, we're here to help with two things:

1. **Real, practical education** — cat behavior, health, nutrition, and everyday care, explained simply.
2. **Curated product recommendations** — items we've researched, tested, or personally use with Millie, complete with honest pros, cons, and specs.

We believe good information and good products shouldn't be hard to find. That's the whole point of this site.

Thanks for stopping by — and give your cat a scratch behind the ears for us.
